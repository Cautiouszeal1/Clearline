    Before you start: deletions are permanent. Download your X archive first (Settings → Your account → Download an archive of your data). Clearline is an unofficial tool — automated actions may go against X's rules and X can rate-limit or temporarily lock accounts that act too fast. Use a desktop browser and keep the delay sensible.

    How it works

    The script does exactly what you'd do by hand — just tirelessly.

    Runs in your tab

    You paste the script into your own logged-in x.com tab. Nothing is sent to Clearline or anyone else.

    Clicks like a human

    It scrolls your timeline and presses the same Delete, Undo repost and Unlike buttons you would, with randomised pauses.

    You stay in control

    A live panel on x.com shows progress, and a Stop button halts it instantly. Refresh the page to cancel at any time.

    FAQ

     

    Why two pages — profile and likes?

    X shows your posts, replies and reposts on one tab and your likes on another. Run the same script on each page; it detects where it is and does the right job.

    Will it get all my old likes?

    Your Likes tab only loads a limited history (often a few thousand). Very old likes that no longer appear there can't be reached this way — run it again after a day, or re-like and unlike a post to refresh it.

    How are replies told apart from posts?

    Replies are detected by X's "Replying to" label or by sitting under someone else's post in a thread. It's best-effort; if you want to be strict, turn on Dry run first and check the counts.

    It stopped halfway.

    That's usually a rate limit or the timeline running out of loaded items. The script pauses for 5 minutes if X shows an error toast. Otherwise just refresh and paste it again — it picks up where the timeline starts.

    Chrome won't let me paste into the console.

    Chrome asks you to type allow pasting the first time. Type it, press Enter, then paste again. Only ever paste scripts you trust into a console.

    Can I use it on my phone?

    No — it needs a desktop browser's developer console (Chrome, Edge, Brave or Firefox). Build the script here on your phone and open it on your computer, or do the whole thing on desktop.
