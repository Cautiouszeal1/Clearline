FAQ

 

Do I need to type my username?

No. Clearline reads it from the X page you're logged in to.

I clicked the bookmark and nothing happened.

Make sure you're on x.com and logged in, then refresh and click it again. If your browser blocks it, use the console option under Settings.

Will it get all my old likes?

X's Likes tab only loads a limited history (often a few thousand). Very old likes that don't appear there can't be reached. Running it again a day later often reaches more.

It stopped before finishing.

Usually a rate limit or the timeline ran out of loaded items. Clearline rests 5 minutes if X shows an error. Otherwise refresh x.com and click the bookmark again; it picks up from where your timeline starts.

How does it tell replies from posts?

From X's "Replying to" label or from sitting under someone else's post in a thread. It's best-effort. Use Dry run to check the counts first if that matters to you.

Can I use it on my phone?

It's built for desktop browsers. Phones make bookmarklets awkward and X's mobile site behaves differently.
